Tim Berners Lee's Original Research Concerning Physics and the Current Uses of the World Wide Web

Tim Berners Lee's Original Research Concerning Physics

Tim Berners-Lee created the World Wide Web while working at CERN with the intention for sharing information more easily for people. He noticed that other scientist had a problem keeping track of their research, documents, and updates because data was stored on different incompatible computers and systems. He changed that so everyone would be able to share data, information, research, documents, updates, etc around the world in one place. His main goal was to make it easy for scientist. However, now people use it for everything.

the Current Uses of the World Wide Web

Today, the World Wide Web is used for much more than scientific research. People use it for education, communication, entertainment, online shopping, banking, social media, etc. The web now connects billions of people worldwide for everything and not just for research and scientists. However, the purpose is still the same. Time Berners Lee created the WWW to make sharing information on the internet around the world possible for people and people around world currently share information, post, stories, etc on the internet.

Inventor of the Web: Tim Berners Lee

Tim Berners Lee is a computer scientist known for inventing the World Wide Web in 1989. His inventions included HTML (HyperText Markup Language), HTTP (HyperText Transfer Protocol), and URLs (Uniform Resource Locators). His invention made sharing information on the internet around the world possible for people. He was working at CERN when he invented the WWW. He created the WWW because during his work, he noticed that other scientist had a problem keeping track of their research, documents, and updates because data was stored on different incompatible computers and systems. He changed that so everyone would be able to share data, information, research, documents, updates, etc around the world in one place.
